Ask a question
What should I bring?

Swimwear, a towel, sunscreen (reef-safe if you can), sunglasses and a hat. For the sunset party, bring a light layer for the evening breeze.

Is it suitable for children?

Families are welcome on our island day trips and private charters. Let us know the children's ages when you book so we can prepare. For the Friday sunset party, please ask us first.

What if the weather is bad?

Your safety comes first. If the sea or weather isn't suitable, we'll contact you to move your trip to another day or arrange a refund.

Can I book a private boat?

Yes. You can book the whole catamaran for birthdays, families, groups or events. Message us on WhatsApp with your date and number of guests and we'll send you a quote.

How do I pay?

Book on WhatsApp and we'll confirm your spot together with the payment options. You'll know exactly what to pay and how before the day of your trip.
